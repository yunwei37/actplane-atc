# MLSys 2027 submission format (Research Track)

Source: https://mlsys.org/Conferences/2027/CallForResearchPapers (read 2026-10-29). OpenReview submission #369.

## Deadlines (UTC)

- Paper, appendix and author list are due **2026-10-30 20:00 UTC**. The OpenReview form closes at 20:30 UTC, but the call's deadline is 20:00.
- Author lists **cannot change after the deadline**. Per-author submission quotas are counted from the lists as they stand at the deadline.

## Main paper

- Two-column format, **at most 10 pages not including references**.
- Use the official style files, `mlsys2025style.zip` ("we are using the same submission format as 2025"). This repository uses `mlsys2025.sty` in its review mode, i.e. without `[accepted]`, which prints "Anonymous Authors".
- **Every reference must explicitly list all authors.** Do not write "and others" or "et al." in `references.bib`.
- **Mandatory "AI Usage Disclosure" section, placed after the references.** It does not count toward the page limit. Describe which tools were used, for which parts of the work, and to what extent. Content generated by LLMs alone is prohibited unless it is illustrative or part of the experimental analysis.

## Appendix

- Unlimited pages, but reviewers are not required to read it.
- **Upload it as a separate PDF**, not appended to the main paper. It is due at the same time as the main paper and can be uploaded after the paper.

## Anonymity (double blind; non-compliant papers are rejected without review)

- Avoid identifying the authors explicitly or implicitly, including through self-references and acknowledgments.
- **Institutional affiliations must be anonymized**, both in the author block and in the text.
- Because a public arXiv or open-source version exists, use a different system name and title from that version. This paper uses "AgPlane", not ActPlane, and must contain no GitHub links to the public repository.
- Also check PDF metadata and embedded file names. `main.tex` sets `\pdfsuppressptexinfo=-1` for this.

## OpenReview

- Every author needs a complete, active OpenReview profile. Every author must register their conflicts.
- At least one author must sign up as a reciprocal reviewer (here ~YUSHENG_ZHENG1).
- Author entries must be profile IDs, and each author's institution must match an institution in that author's profile.
- Editing the submission resends the full content plus the license.

## Other

- No dual submission while the paper is under review at another conference. Technical reports such as arXiv are allowed.
- Artifact evaluation is voluntary and only for accepted papers.
- The call names no font requirement, but avoid Type 3 fonts in figures (matplotlib `pdf.fonttype=42`) to be safe.
